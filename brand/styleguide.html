Contrast

Cyan is a surface colour, never a text colour

#1EC4F4 on white measures 2.05:1. White on cyan measures the same. Both fail AA for body and large text. Navy on cyan passes at 5.89:1 — that is why the navigation works today. Keep nav links navy, never white.

What to fix, in order

  1. Cyan text fails contrast at 2.05:1. Anywhere cyan is text on white, or white is text on cyan, it is unreadable for a real share of customers.
  2. Two yellows. #FED200 everywhere, but the form Submit is #FED203 on #373737. Consolidate.
  3. Roboto is loading alongside Montserrat on the live site. Theme leakage — remove.
  4. #6EC1E4 is an Elementor default blue, not a brand colour, and fails at 2.02:1.
  5. Button text at 12–13px, weight split 500 / 600 across builds. Standardise at 15px / 600.
  6. Six border radii. Reduce to four.
  7. No H3 tier — the scale falls from 42px straight to 19px.
  8. Three simultaneous primary CTAs in one viewport.
  9. Logo has a baked-in background. Needs three transparent SVGs.
  10. Icons are 60×60 PNGs. Convert to SVG at one stroke weight in navy.
